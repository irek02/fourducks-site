Attio for Sheets

by Four Ducks

Attio for Sheets brings your Attio CRM into Google Sheets.

A Google Workspace Add-on by Four Ducks. Connect your Attio account, then import People and Companies straight into a spreadsheet — with one-click refresh and an optional daily auto-sync.

What it does

  • Connect securely to Attio with OAuth — no copy-pasting API keys.
  • Import People and Companies into named tabs in your active spreadsheet.
  • Refresh on demand, or enable a daily auto-refresh.
  • Read-only against your Attio workspace — the add-on never writes back to Attio.

How it works

  1. Install the add-on and open the sidebar in Google Sheets.
  2. Click Connect Attio and authorize.
  3. Pick People or Companies and click Import.
  4. Use Refresh any time, or turn on daily auto-refresh.
Your data stays yours.

The add-on stores only your Attio access token and your import preferences — all inside your own Google account. There is no external server or database. See the Privacy Policy for details.
