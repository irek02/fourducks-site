6. Apps Script resource limits

The Add-on runs inside the Google Apps Script trigger budget of the Google account in which it is installed. Each linked database uses approximately 32 minutes of that daily budget at the default 5-minute sync interval. A standard consumer Google account provides approximately 90 minutes per day, which supports roughly three linked databases. This is an architectural constraint of the Google Apps Script platform, not a policy restriction, and Four Ducks accepts no liability for sync interruptions caused by quota exhaustion.
